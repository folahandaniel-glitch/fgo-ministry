About Fodan Gospel Outreach

Banjo Folahan Daniel (King Fodan)
Presiding Apostle & Founder, Fodan Gospel Outreach

Founder of The Living Youth International and Founding Senior Minister and General Overseer of The Living Christian Sanctuary. A Psalmist and Prophetic Pastor called to raise youth, strengthen families, and disciple generations for Christ.

Our Mission

Fodan Gospel Outreach exists to raise a generation that walks in the power and character of Christ. Through evangelism, discipleship, and hands-on ministry across every stage of life, from children to the elderly, we are committed to seeing families strengthened, communities transformed, and disciples sent out to carry the Gospel with fire, light, and lasting fruit.
